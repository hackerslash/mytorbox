Stats

Rolling 30-day window

Counters live under the st:* Redis namespace, every key with a ~31-day TTL — the window prunes itself. Users are identified only by a SHA-256 of their key triple; daily uniques use HyperLogLog, so counts are approximate (±0.8%). Writes are buffered in-process and flushed on a timer (STATS_FLUSH_SECONDS), and this payload is cached for 60s (Refresh forces a recompute).
